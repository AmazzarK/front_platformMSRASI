## Brand & Style

The design system bridges Moroccan heritage and high-rigor academic science. It communicates institutional authority, global research credibility, and dynamic technological progress. Designed for researchers, academic partners, policy leaders, and international scholars, the interface commands trust while remaining open and forward-looking.

### Visual Style: Editorial Institutionalism
The design rejects cold corporate austerity in favor of an **Editorial Institutionalism** aesthetic:
- **Structural Precision:** Clean grid alignment influenced by mathematical topology, scientific publications, and balanced geometric ratios.
- **Moroccan & Scientific Accents:** Deep regal blues provide a steady base, balanced by vibrant bursts of Moroccan green, scientific red, and innovation orange for peer-reviewed statuses, callouts, and key data points.
- **Tactile Editorial Clarity:** Research cards emulate refined academic monographs, with crisp borders, airy leading, and defined metadata badges.
- **Graphic Network Motifs:** Faint vector node-link patterns and subtle geometric watermarks may be used sparingly on headers and hero banners to symbolize networked research clusters across Morocco and abroad.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative blues with culturally grounded, functional accents designed to distinguish academic tracks, review workflows, and publication tiers.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Royal Blue (`#1646A8`):** The primary brand anchor for key actions, top-level navigation, and active interactive states.
- **Deep Institutional Navy (`#10243B`):** Structural backdrops, high-level headers, and dark surfaces for executive dashboards and symposium banners.
- **Secondary Blue (`#2466C5`):** Secondary controls, interactive text links, and focused input indicators.
- **Innovation Orange (`#F58200`):** Action-driving tertiary accent for calls to submit papers, awards, innovation metrics, and highlighted events.
- **Moroccan Green (`#087A4B`):** Peer-reviewed approval states, verified institutional badge marks, sustainable science initiatives, and success messaging.
- **Scientific Red (`#D62828`):** Deadlines, urgent notifications, critical review alerts, and negative statuses.

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- **Canvas White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base layer for papers, main articles, and reading surfaces.
- **Soft Background (`#F5F7FB`):** Ambient canvas for multi-card dashboard arrays and administrative portals.
- **Light Blue Surface (`#EAF2FC`):** Callout boxes, selected table rows, active badge backdrops, and secondary card containers.
- **Main Text (`#18263A`):** Maximum contrast charcoal-navy for headlines and body text, strictly exceeding WCAG AAA standards.
- **Secondary Text (`#64748B`):** Slate tone for metadata, citations, journal volumes, and timestamp labels.
- **Border Gray (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px borders for structural cards, data table dividers, and input outlines.

## Typography

The typographic system creates clear contrast between modern geometric headings (**Manrope**) and highly legible, neutral body text (**Inter**).

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (Manrope):** Geometric structure with humanistic curves, delivering a balance of academic rigor and technological innovation. Large headlines employ tight negative tracking to maintain punch and readability.
- **Narratives & Scientific Abstracts (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible across long-form research papers, multi-column abstracts, and dense data layouts.
- **Badges, Metrics & Metadata:** Set in semibold and bold weights of `Inter` with slightly increased letter spacing (`0.04em`) to ensure instant scannability on badges, DOI links, and publication issue tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a multi-tier responsive grid system built on an 8px base rhythm.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, max content width 1360px, `gutter-desktop` (32px), `margin-desktop` (48px). Accommodates research sidebars, directory filters, and multi-tier paper grids.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (32px). Sidebars collapse into expandable filter drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter-mobile` (16px), `margin-mobile` (20px). Full horizontal bleed for action sheets with edge-to-edge data cards.

### Layout Principles
- **Macro Whitespace:** Research sections and scientific tracks are separated by `space-xl` (40px) or double increments to retain editorial calm.
- **Micro Rhythms:** Metadata chips, DOI badges, author lists, and inline controls strictly adhere to `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), and `space-md` (16px) step units to maintain layout integrity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal surfaces and subtle, cool-tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding artificial high-offset elevations.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Level (Canvas):** `#F5F7FB` provides a comfortable, low-glare canvas for long viewing periods.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Research Cards & Interactive Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` with heightened ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs & Active Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.04)`. Supported by a dark backdrop overlay: `rgba(16, 36, 59, 0.6)` with a `backdrop-blur(4px)` treatment.
- **Recessed Surface (Data Tables & Well Insets):** `#EAF2FC` or `#F8FAFC` recessed containers with an inset border and zero elevation, grounding sub-sections and scientific methodology panels.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Level 2 (Rounded)** scale, striking an intentional balance between institutional structure and modern polish.

### Shape Scale Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `rounded` (8px / 0.5rem). Provides functional precision with softened touch targets.
- **Containers & Editorial Cards:** `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem). Framed with thin 1px `#E2E8F0` borders to form clean, organized modules.
- **Hero Containers, Event Panels & Modals:** `rounded-xl` (24px / 1.5rem). Softens large surface areas without looking overly casual.
- **Pill Form Elements (Status Chips, Event Dates, Categories):** Fully rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for metadata tokens, author avatars, and publication badges.

## Components

### 1. Primary, Secondary & Accent Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1646A8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded` (8px), font `label-md`. Hover state: `#2466C5`. Focus ring: 3px `#EAF2FC` outline around `#1646A8`.
- **Secondary / Scientific Accent:** Solid `#087A4B` background with `#FFFFFF` text for submission milestones; solid `#F58200` background for symposium registrations.
- **Outline Action:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#18263A` text. Hover state: `#EAF2FC` background with `#1646A8` text and border.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `#1646A8` text. Hover state: `#EAF2FC` background.

### 2. Editorial Research & Event Cards
- **Editorial Research Card:** `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-lg` corners and 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Includes a 4px vertical accent stripe on the left edge indicating field discipline (e.g., `#087A4B` for Life Sciences, `#1646A8` for Engineering, `#F58200` for Innovation). Contains title (`headline-xs`), authors line (`body-sm`), DOI link badge, and download CTA.
- **Multi-Tiered Event Card:** Dual-zone layout. Left/top area uses a `#10243B` dark block highlighting the date and location in `#FFFFFF`. The adjacent content area (`#FFFFFF`) holds the title, topic tags, agenda preview, and registration action.

### 3. Chips & Badge Tags
- **Event / Category Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`.
  - *Applied Science:* `#EAF2FC` background, `#1646A8` text.
  - *Innovation Initiative:* `#FFF4E5` background, `#F58200` text.
  - *Peer-Reviewed / Active:* `#E6F5EE` background, `#087A4B` text.
  - *Call for Papers / Urgent:* `#FDE8E8` background, `#D62828` text.

### 4. Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded` (8px), typography `body-md`.
- **Placeholder:** `#64748B`.
- **Active / Focused:** 1px `#2466C5` border accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(36, 102, 197, 0.15)` glow.
- **Scientific Keyword Search:** Input field integrated with a prepended search glyph, dynamic badge insertion for filters, and a trailing primary button.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** 18px × 18px square with 4px border radius. Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border. Checked: `#1646A8` background with an integrated white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Button:** 18px circular control. Checked: 2px `#1646A8` border enclosing a centered 8px solid `#1646A8` circular dot.

### 6. Data Tables (Administration & Publication Archives)
- **Header:** `#F5F7FB` background, 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm` in `#64748B` with uppercase tracking.
- **Row Anatomy:** `#FFFFFF` background, minimum height 52px, alternating or clean grid separated by 1px `#E2E8F0`. Hover state triggers `#EAF2FC` across the entire row.
- **Cell Alignment:** Numerical scientific data and dates right-aligned; manuscript titles, authors, and tracking codes left-aligned.

### 7. Administration UI Panel & Navigation
- **Sidebar Nav:** `#10243B` deep institutional navy background, typography `headline-xs` and `body-sm`. Active item marked by `#1646A8` pill container and a 3px `#F58200` left indicator bar.
- **Metric Cards:** `#FFFFFF` card with `rounded-lg` containing large numerical indicators (`headline-lg` in `#10243B`), secondary progress delta labels (`+14% this quarter` in `#087A4B`), and subtle top right node-link icon motifs.
